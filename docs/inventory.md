# Inventory, drops, and buying

Press **G** to toss the held firearm from hand height in the aiming direction. It carries some player movement, tumbles through a short arc, and settles on the ground. Knives stay equipped. Walk over a nearby gun to fill an empty primary or sidearm slot; press **E** near a gun to exchange the occupied slot. A use press that picks up a weapon does not also start a bomb action. The world pickup preserves its magazine and reserve ammunition, and switching away/back retains those values. Death drops carried firearms. Bots can recover nearby, visible primary weapons when their current weapon is inferior and they are not actively fighting or reloading. These interactions enable giving a bot a weapon and recovering an opponent's gun.

Dropped guns use existing local world models and bounded gravity/collision updates. Settled guns sleep instead of repeating collision work at 128 Hz; airborne motion and pickup-distance checks reuse scratch vectors. The scene holds at most 64 drops, clears them at round/map resets, and disposes transforms without disposing shared weapon templates. Physics, pickup radius and bot weapon valuation are browser approximations; there is no native dropped-weapon rigid-body or skin-ownership simulation. Paint follows the existing locally selected weapon appearance.

Purchasing a replacement firearm drops the previous slot's gun. Unused guns bought during the current round can be refunded while buying is allowed. Only eligible purchases show a small back-arrow refund button on their card; equipment and grenades use the same control. Knives are excluded from buying and selected with Equip Knife in Weapon Skins. Shooting, dropping, picking up, death and the next round invalidate applicable receipts. Refunds return the actual amount paid and remove the item. A zero-cost practice purchase does not create money.

Equipment refunds restore the exact prior armor/helmet/kit state. For a vest followed by a helmet upgrade, refund the helmet upgrade first, then the vest. Damage invalidates armor receipts; using a kit to defuse invalidates its receipt. Kit receipts are independent of armor receipts. Free armor or a kit received from another round has no purchase receipt and cannot be refunded. Grenades also have same-round, unthrown purchase receipts; see [utility](utility.md).

The main menu’s **LOADOUT** button opens faction-specific buy-slot selection. Five pistols, five mid-tier guns and five rifles can be assigned; only faction-available weapons appear. Choices persist locally and casual/competitive rounds snapshot their selection at round start, so editing during a round applies next round and cannot bypass that round’s slot limit. Purchase checks enforce this selection in both modes; practice keeps the full roster. There is no network inventory service.

Validation: `bun run test:browser tests/inventory.spec.ts` exercises drop/pickup ammo preservation, vacated sidearm slots, weapon refunds, restricted competitive purchases, reverse-order equipment refunds, invalidation after armor damage and kit use. It also captures a settled world-gun screenshot and the loadout editor to ignored `artifacts/`. The same integration uses the actual native Dust II buy-zone positions for buy eligibility.

## Inventory and buy slots

Open **INVENTORY** from the main menu. Select T or CT, choose one of five slots in Pistols, Mid-Tier or Rifles, then choose its weapon. Replacing with an already selected weapon swaps their positions. Choices are saved locally for both casual and competitive, with an ordered snapshot taken at round start. In-match edits apply next round. Practice keeps the full armory.

The buy screen places category columns to the left and the current team’s agent to the right. Hover or focus a firearm or grenade to see it held by the agent. Equipment previews use an unarmed pose. This uses the bundled models with native buy-menu poses, fingers and secondary weapon tracks. The weapon origin and mesh bind frame drive placement; no bounding-box centering or generic pistol grip is used for grenades. See [native buy poses](buy-poses.md) for extraction details and remaining retargeting limits. Refund arrows appear in the bottom-left corner of eligible purchases.
